# Master Your Career Path — Email Templates (v1)

**Created:** 12 November 2026 · **Status:** DRAFT for review — nothing is live or scheduled.  
**Goals:** sell coaching and courses (PRIME, OPERATE) · build authority and audience · grow paid subscriptions.  
**Voice:** punchy, direct, human. First person, written like a letter. UK English.  
**How to use:** anything in \[SQUARE BRACKETS\] is a slot to fill before sending. Slots marked YOUR STORY must be your own real experience — do not send invented anecdotes under your name.

## Rules for every email

- Plain-text feel: no banners, short paragraphs, one idea per email.
- One link or button per email, repeated at most twice.
- Teach first, sell second. The offer only appears from welcome email 4 onwards.
- Any results you quote (salary rises, promotions) must be real and provable.

## Welcome sequence — 5 emails over 10 days

Day 0 → Day 2 → Day 4 → Day 7 → Day 10. Subscribers in the sequence should not also receive sales emails until it ends.

### Welcome 1 (Day 0) — Welcome and one question

**Job:** set expectations, get a reply.  
**Subject:** Welcome to The Pathway — where are you right now?

I'm Didi. I was a Commercial Contracts Manager before I turned thirty, and I later left employment to go contracting. Along the way I learned that getting a role and thriving in it are two completely different skills — and that most career advice only covers the first.

That's what this newsletter is about. Each \[SEND DAY\] you'll get one clear idea you can use at work that week. No fluff, no motivational posters.

**One question before we start:** hit reply and tell me which of these is you right now.

- **A** — I'm trying to land a new role.
- **B** — I've got the role, and I want to do well in it.
- **C** — I'm not sure what I want next.

Just the letter is fine. I read every reply, and it tells me what to write about.

Speak soon,  
Didi
