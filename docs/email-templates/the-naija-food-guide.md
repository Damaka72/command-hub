# The Naija Food Guide — Email Templates (v1)

**Created:** 12 November 2026 · **Status:** DRAFT for review — nothing is live or scheduled. The site and newsletter are not yet launched.  
**Goals:** build a launch waitlist · drive restaurant discovery · bring readers back for recipes and food content · earn from paid listings and adverts.  
**Voice:** warm, proud, appetising, and specific — name the dish, name the place. UK English.  
**How to use:** anything in \[SQUARE BRACKETS\] is a slot to fill before sending.

## Rules for every email

- Lead with food: one strong photo near the top of every email.
- One main button per email.
- Paid placements are always labelled "Featured partner" or "Sponsored".
- Only use photos you have the right to use.
- Emails to restaurant owners are business-to-business; keep them short and personal, and always offer a way to opt out.
