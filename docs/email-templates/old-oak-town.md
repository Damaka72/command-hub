# Old Oak Town — Email Templates (v1)

**Created:** 12 November 2026 · **Status:** DRAFT for review — nothing is live or scheduled.  
**Goals:** grow local readership · sell local sponsorship · drive traffic to oldoaktown.co.uk · build community and events.  
**Voice:** community-first, factual, local. UK English. Sign-off: "Your neighbourhood, in your inbox."  
**How to use:** anything in \[SQUARE BRACKETS\] is a slot to fill before sending. Nothing sends with an unfilled slot.

## Rules for every email

- One main job per email, one main button (repeated at most twice).
- Single column, short paragraphs, readable on a phone.
- Sponsored content is always labelled "Sponsored" or "In partnership with".
- Add a postal address to the Beehiiv email footer (currently blank — needed for compliance).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Welcome email

**When:** immediately on sign-up. **Job:** set expectations and get one reply.

**Subject:** Welcome to The Oak — one quick question  
**Preview:** What lands in your inbox, when, and how to shape it.

Hello, and welcome to The Oak.

I'm Didi, and I write this from right here in Old Oak. Every week you'll get one email that tells you, in plain English, what is actually happening on your doorstep: the station, the planning decisions, the new businesses, and what's on this weekend.

**What to expect**

- **One email a week**, every \[SEND DAY\]. No daily noise.
- **The big local story**, explained without the planning jargon.
- **From the directory:** local businesses worth knowing about.
- **Your weekend:** events and things to do nearby.

**One quick question.** Hit reply and tell me: which street or part of the area are you in, and what do you most want covered? I read every reply, and it shapes what goes in.

While you're here, the full business directory and past stories are on the site:

**\[BUTTON: Explore Old Oak Town → https://oldoaktown.co.uk\]**

Until \[SEND DAY\],  
Didi  
*Your neighbourhood, in your inbox.*

P.S. Replying also tells your email provider you want to hear from us, which keeps The Oak out of your spam folder.
