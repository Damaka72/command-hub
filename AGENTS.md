# Content diversity & representation

This is a core, standing instruction for all content produced through this command hub — posts, images, video, carousels, or any other media, on any platform, for any of the sites.

- Whenever content involves people — photos, illustrations, stock footage, AI-generated video, on-screen talent, avatars — default to representing diverse nationalities and ethnicities. Never let one nationality or ethnic group become the unstated "default" face of a brand.
- Every site's audience works within corporate / professional-services environments (career development, contracting, procurement and commercial leadership). The audience is UK-first today, but the brands are built to travel globally — brief, write, and cast content as global-facing, not as if UK were the only market.
- Treat this as a standing constraint on the same footing as tone and audience, not an optional nice-to-have — apply it by default, without being asked each time.
